        Controls

        A key per kick; how long you hold it sets the power – your player winds up while you hold, so the ball is struck the moment you let go. On a ball coming to you, press twice to fill the gauge twice as fast, three times for full power (a big first-time clearance). Click a key to rebind it. (Pad and touch: a target button – team-mate, run path, goal, self – and a height.)

        Targets: Team-mate plays it to where he is now, Run path into the space ahead of him, Goal shoots, Self plays it to yourself (on the keyboard: Q / A / S through, lob and ground passes, D shoot, W knock it on, F lift it, E control it). The stick picks which team-mate (or where the shot goes). Heights: ground = along the grass to his feet (a low shot), mid = chest-high (a driven ball), high = head-high for a header (a shot under the bar; little power + high = a chip).

        Shooting: it goes where the stick points – the spot on the goal line along it, up to a metre wide of a post (bend it back in with aftertouch). Power is pace, not height – a full-power shot is hard to keep down and less accurate, and the further out you shoot from, the wider it can go; every shot swerves a little – bend it back with aftertouch. A high shot goes in just under the bar. R2 + Goal = finesse (curled). When the ball is in the air, a Goal key volleys, heads or – facing away – bicycle-kicks it.

        Back to goal: a Goal key turns with the ball and shoots – full power, but the turn takes a moment (quicker for a small, nimble player) and you can be tackled. Close in, a quick tap back-heels it instead – at once, but weak. A ball in the air: an overhead kick (flick it up yourself with Self-high first).

        Self: ground = knock it on and chase it – a tap just nudges it (sideways / back on the stick: step-over feint, drag-back), mid = pop it up to chest height, high = flick it up / scoop it over a challenge – and pressed again as it comes down, keepy-ups (foot, knee / thigh, chest; held long, the head). When the ball comes to you, it controls it towards the stick with foot, chest or head. Control (E): a ball coming taken dead at your feet – chest, thigh, foot, or a jump to head it down – as the keeper in his box, hold it (or R2) to catch: without it a ball at you only hits you. Tricks (R, pad R3, touch ✦): at your feet an elastico (stick to a side), la croqueta (diagonally ahead), a back-heel flick (back) or a rainbow flick (forward); a ball dropping with the stick pushed, a sombrero over the man in front; juggling, around the world (press as it drops past your knee) or a neck stall (press a metre above your neck; F pops it off) – timed: a bubble says perfect, good or mistimed. R2 with a pass key: a no-look pass. X / Z held with a kick key: a level higher / lower (Z + D a low shot, Shift + D or X + D the top corner, X + A to his head).

        Aftertouch (Sensible Soccer style): right after a kick, push the stick left/right of the ball's flight to bend it that way, back to lift it, forward to dip it. Keep holding your aim and nothing happens – turn the stick, or let go and press again.

        Shooting practice: a new room has an AI keeper in Team B's goal; in the warm-up with only AI keepers on the pitch besides you, the keepers serve every ball back to you – no kick-offs, set pieces or goal celebrations. Any other warm-up is played by the rules like a match, without a clock or a score. Click outside the room panel (or press Esc) to tuck it away.

        Defence: hold a Team-mate key to press the ball carrier (auto standing tackle), hold a Self key to jockey (stay goal-side at arm's length, facing him, no lunge), hold R2 to guard (keep facing the ball, side-stepping into position), Goal = slide tackle (from behind may be a foul; keeper: dive), Run path = send your keeper out. When a shot or pass comes at you, press any kick key to block it – your player throws out a leg, his body or jumps to head it (the keeper uses his hands). The touch buttons and an on-screen hint switch to these whenever you're defending.

        Keeper with the ball: the ground pass rolls it out, the lob pass throws it (a drop kick when it's far), shoot punts it.

        Passing past opponents: a ground pass is angled round an opponent near its line when your team-mate can still get to it. Otherwise it goes as close past him as it can: played straight at him it's usually stopped (now and then it goes through his legs), just past his foot it gets by more often.

        Team-mates: a Team-mate or Run key while an AI team-mate has the ball calls for it – your keeper too.

        Receive assist: when the ball is played to you your player is guided to meet it; a ring on the grass marks the spot (yellow = header height). Hold a kick key as it arrives for a first-time pass, volley or header.

        High balls – how you meet it decides what happens. The assist keeps you on the ball's line; you choose with the stick. Run at the ball (stick back along its flight): with a kick key, a header as high as you can jump; with no key, you cushion it on the head or chest. Run with it (stick along its flight): with a kick key, a volley if you get there in time, otherwise a flying header (facing away from goal, a bicycle kick); with no key, you take it on the thigh or foot, in your stride. Neither: you take it at whatever height it arrives. Your choice holds until the ball is touched again.

        Touch: drag anywhere on the left half for the stick – you run (and dribble) at full pace; R1 (Get ball) runs to the ball or closes down whoever has it. On the right are eight round buttons, laid out as the keyboard's keys – Through, Knock on, Control, Trick over Lob, Pass, Shoot, Lift: slide the finger up on one for a level higher (further, two – the top corner), down for a level lower; how long you hold sets the power.

        Quick chat: keys 1–5 (or 💬 on touch) – Pass! · Shoot! · Nice one! · Sorry! · Good game!
